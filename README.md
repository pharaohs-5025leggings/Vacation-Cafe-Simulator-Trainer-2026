## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Vacation Cafe Simulator**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for managing the cafe, preparing food, handling ingredients, exploring the coastal world, and progressing through upgrades and reputation milestones.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed cafe-management experience, faster access to upgrades and decorations, easier cooking and resource gathering, or a creative sandbox for building their ideal Italian cafe.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Money** — Prevents cafe funds from decreasing when purchasing equipment, menu improvements, or interior decorations.
- **Unlimited Ingredients** — Keeps cooking ingredients available while preparing pizza, pasta, bruschetta, coffee, pastries, and other cafe products.
- **Max Reputation** — Sets the cafe reputation value to a high level to accelerate customer growth and business progression.
- **Instant Cooking** — Completes supported food-preparation steps immediately during the game's cooking mini-games.
- **Easy Exploration** — Supports relaxed exploration of the coastal town, shops, farms, wineries, and underwater ingredient-gathering areas.
- **Co-op Safety Mode** — Provides a reminder to use trainer options only in private sessions and disables risky features when multiplayer synchronization is detected.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **No Item Consumption** — Stops ingredients and collected resources from being removed during cooking, serving, or other valid uses.
- **Unlimited Collected Resources** — Prevents supported gathered resources, including underwater ingredients such as oysters, from being depleted.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Combat** — Vacation Cafe Simulator has no combat system, weapons, enemies, or combat encounters.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore the coastal vacation town on foot and use the Vespa to travel between available locations. The trainer does not add new movement systems; its exploration options are intended to reduce resource limitations while traveling around the world.

### Tools & Inventory

Ingredients and gathered resources support cooking and cafe operations. Activate unlimited ingredients or no item consumption before preparing dishes or collecting materials. Avoid changing inventory values during loading screens or while joining a co-op session.

### World & Environment

The game combines cafe management with exploration. Visit shops, farms, wineries, and underwater areas to find opportunities and fresh ingredients. Use economy and reputation options to accelerate upgrades, menu development, and business growth.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no combat gameplay in Vacation Cafe Simulator. The trainer therefore provides no weapon, health, ammunition, enemy, or combat-related options.
